# Sources

Every source cited in [`content/curriculum-plan.yaml`](../content/curriculum-plan.yaml) is registered in
[`content/sources/core.yaml`](../content/sources/core.yaml). All verification below was done on
**2026-11-14**. "Verified" means the page or PDF was fetched on that date and the cited section
heading was found in it and is about the topic it is cited for. It does **not** mean every
claim a future lesson makes has been checked. Lesson authors must still open the section and
confirm their specific claims (CONTENT_GUIDE.md, "Never cite from memory").

## Licence and attribution policy

All lessons are original prose. No source text is copied into the app. Sources are linked,
not redistributed. Licences recorded in `core.yaml` are those stated by the source where
known. Where the licence was not checked, the entry says only "linked, not redistributed" and
claims no specific licence. CC BY / CC BY-SA sources (System Design Primer, Systems Approach,
OWASP, Kubernetes docs) should still be attributed by title and link in the lesson's sources
list, which the app displays.
